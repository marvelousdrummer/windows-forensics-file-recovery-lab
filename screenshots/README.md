# Screenshot review checklist

The original Week 1 experiment produced 26 screenshots, but **none are included in this starter package** until they are manually reviewed.

Before uploading a screenshot:

1. Remove or blur local Windows usernames, account avatars, unique machine names, and personal directory paths if they appear.
2. Crop out unrelated applications, messages, notifications, and other personal information.
3. Do not include copyrighted textbook pages, EC-Council lab instructions/screenshots, or University of Phoenix assignment text.
4. Confirm the screenshot shows your own experiment and matches its caption.
5. Do not publish actual recovered documents or virtual disk images unless specifically vetted.

The file list and intended subjects appear in `../docs/evidence-register.csv`.
